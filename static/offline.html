Maize (Ibigori) Season A / B

  • Best time to plant: Sept 15 to Oct 20 (Season A)
  • Soil acidity: pH 5.5 to 6.8 (consider lime below 5.2)
  • Fertilizer guide: 100 kg/ha DAP at planting, 50 kg/ha Urea at knee-high
  • Look out for: Fall Armyworm; ask a local agronomist about treatment at the first signs
  • Typical Rwanda yield: 2.2 to 3.8 MT/ha

Climbing Beans (Ibishyimbo) Season A / B

  • Best time to plant: Sept 25 to Oct 30
  • Soil acidity: pH 5.8 to 6.5
  • Staking: 2 to 2.5m stakes at 2 weeks post-emergence
  • Fertilizer guide: NPK 17-17-17 (50 kg/ha) + rhizobium inoculant
  • Look out for: Anthracnose and angular leaf spot; give plants room to breathe
  • Typical Rwanda yield: 1.5 to 2.4 MT/ha

Irish Potato (Ibirayi) Volcanic / High

  • Common growing areas: Musanze, Nyabihu, Rubavu, Burera
  • Typical elevation: 1,800 to 2,600m
  • Fertilizer guide: NPK 17-17-17 (300 kg/ha) at planting
  • Look out for: Late blight; ask an agronomist about prevention
  • Typical Rwanda yield: 12.0 to 18.5 MT/ha

Rice (Umuceri) Marshland

  • Common growing areas: Bugarama, Rwamagana, Bugesera, Kirehe
  • Water needs: Keep the field moist from transplanting until the heads form
  • Fertilizer guide: Basal NPK + split Urea (tillering & panicle initiation)
  • Typical Rwanda yield: 4.5 to 6.2 MT/ha

Cassava (Imyumbati) Perennial

  • Common growing areas: Bugesera, Ruhango, Kamonyi, Gatsibo
  • Varieties: CBSD & CMD resistant clones (e.g. NASE 14, Gahene)
  • Spacing: 1m x 1m (10,000 cuttings/ha)
  • Typical Rwanda yield: 14.0 to 22.0 MT/ha

Soybean (Soya) Rotational

  • Common growing areas: Eastern and Southern Provinces
  • Seed treatment: Bradyrhizobium japonicum before planting
  • Soil acidity: pH 6.0 to 6.8
  • Typical Rwanda yield: 1.1 to 1.8 MT/ha
