Maize (Ibigori) Season A / B

  • Optimal Window: Sept 15 to Oct 20 (Season A)
  • Soil pH: 5.5 to 6.8 (Liming if pH < 5.2)
  • Fertilizer: 100 kg/ha DAP at planting, 50 kg/ha Urea at knee-high
  • Pest Watch: Fall Armyworm (FAW): spray neem or Emamectin benzoate at first leaf holes
  • NISR Benchmark: 2.2 to 3.8 MT/ha

Climbing Beans (Ibishyimbo) Season A / B

  • Optimal Window: Sept 25 to Oct 30
  • Soil pH: 5.8 to 6.5
  • Staking: 2 to 2.5m stakes at 2 weeks post-emergence
  • Fertilizer: NPK 17-17-17 (50 kg/ha) + rhizobium inoculant
  • Disease Watch: Anthracnose & Angular leaf spot: ensure spacing
  • NISR Benchmark: 1.5 to 2.4 MT/ha

Irish Potato (Ibirayi) Volcanic / High

  • Priority Zones: Musanze, Nyabihu, Rubavu, Burera
  • Altitude: 1,800 to 2,600m
  • Fertilizer: NPK 17-17-17 (300 kg/ha) at planting
  • Disease Watch: Late Blight (Phytophthora): preventive copper spraying
  • NISR Benchmark: 12.0 to 18.5 MT/ha

Rice (Umuceri) Marshland

  • Priority Zones: Bugarama, Rwamagana, Bugesera, Kirehe
  • Water Regimen: Continuous saturation from transplanting to heading
  • Fertilizer: Basal NPK + split Urea (tillering & panicle initiation)
  • NISR Benchmark: 4.5 to 6.2 MT/ha

Cassava (Imyumbati) Perennial

  • Priority Zones: Bugesera, Ruhango, Kamonyi, Gatsibo
  • Varieties: CBSD & CMD resistant clones (e.g. NASE 14, Gahene)
  • Spacing: 1m x 1m (10,000 cuttings/ha)
  • NISR Benchmark: 14.0 to 22.0 MT/ha

Soybean (Soya) Rotational

  • Priority Zones: Eastern & Southern Provinces
  • Inoculation: Bradyrhizobium japonicum before planting
  • Optimal pH: 6.0 to 6.8
  • NISR Benchmark: 1.1 to 1.8 MT/ha
