# Map boundary data

`rwanda_district_boundaries.geojson` is a lightweight GeoJSON snapshot of Rwanda's 30 district boundaries. It was retrieved from the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR) District Boundary 2022 FeatureServer, with geometry simplified for browser rendering.

Source: <https://gis-server.statistics.gov.rw/server/rest/services/Hosted/District_Boundary_2022/FeatureServer/0>

The Agricultural Segmentation map colors NISR 2022 district polygons by quartile segments calculated from the saved yield observations for one selected crop and season. It does not use the Zones screen's saved predictions or plot the zone registry's coordinates. The color groups are relative comparisons within the selected dataset, not farm-level estimates, risk scores, or claims about every farm in a district.
